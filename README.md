## 👋 About Me

I work where **models meet machines** — turning emerging AI capabilities into executable hardware and efficient systems.

Ph.D. candidate in Integrated Circuit Engineering at **Beihang University (BUAA)**, 2023–present, building AI systems from the model layer down to GPU kernels and circuits.

- 🔬 **Research** — LLM agents for RTL & circuit generation · GPU kernel generation and optimization · computing-in-memory
- 📄 **Publications** — 8 papers including **3 at DAC 2025**, ICAIS 2025, and IEEE AICAS 2023
- 🛠️ **Building** — agentic kernel workflows, compiler × kernel co-evolution, and evidence-first research maps
- 🌱 **Currently** — agent-driven operator production: kernels, compilers, and evaluation harnesses that improve each other
- 🤝 **Open to collaboration** — reach me at [haiyanq@buaa.edu.cn](mailto:haiyanq@buaa.edu.cn)
